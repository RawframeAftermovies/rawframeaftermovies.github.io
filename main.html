About

Based in Ghent, Belgium, we document underground events through the lens of analog video.
Inspired by the raw aesthetics of DIY underground documentaries from the 90s and 00s, we capture events on tape camcorders, embracing the imperfections, grain and grit of analog footage.
Our aim is to capture the raw, unfiltered atmosphere of every event.
